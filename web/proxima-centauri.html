Proxima Centauri

The star nearest to the Sun, 4.2 light-years away: a small red dwarf, too faint to see with the naked eye.

From 2MASS in March 2000 to SPHEREx in July 2026, Proxima Centauri walks across the field. Chronosphere measures its motion from these 27 images: 3.86 arcseconds a year; the catalogue gives 3.86.
