Luhman 16

The nearest pair of brown dwarfs, 6.5 light-years away, found in 2013 by its motion in the images of NASA’s WISE.

From 2MASS in May 1999 to SPHEREx in July 2026, Luhman 16 walks across the field. Chronosphere measures its motion from these 30 images: 2.80 arcseconds a year; the catalogue gives 2.79. In the visits of WISE and NEOWISE the page also finds Luhman 16 by itself, without being told where to look.
